Node definitions and conventions
The identifiers mean:

A: recorded prior antibiotic-exposure profile.

B, B_star, R_B: gender, recorded gender, and gender missingness.

G, G_star, R_G: age group, recorded age group, and age missingness.

C: baseline comorbidity.

D: ADI profile.

H: recorded baseline organism history.

N: recorded baseline nursing-facility visit history.

S: recorded specimen category.

W: ward context.

T: calendar period.

U_B: unobserved shared resistance factors.

U_W: unrecorded AST panel assignment.

U_L: reporting configuration.

Yj1–Yj4: resistance to the four upstream antibiotics.

Yk: resistance to the downstream antibiotic.

R_Y...: corresponding result missingness.

Y..._star: corresponding recorded resistance result.

U_C: unmeasured clinical selection factors, included only in M2.

Throughout, 
R=0 means observed and 
R=1 means missing.
